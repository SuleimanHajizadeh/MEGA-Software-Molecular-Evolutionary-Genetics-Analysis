# Source: Genomic Big Data Analysis & Clinical Interpretation

## Summary
The PDF document in `docs/` (*"DNT Günü - Bioinformatika - Genom Məlumatlarının (Big Data) Analizi və Kliniki İnterpretasiyası"*) is a comprehensive scientific presentation prepared for World DNA Day. It outlines the end-to-end framework for high-throughput genomic data processing, clinical variant interpretation, and computational pipelines in precision medicine.

## Key Themes & Pipeline Stages
1. **Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) Data Architecture**:
   - Transition from raw reads (FASTQ) to alignments (BAM/CRAM) and variant call sets (VCF).
2. **Big Data Processing in Genomics**:
   - Scalable algorithms, quality control metrics (Phred quality scores, depth of coverage), and reference genome alignments (GRCh38).
3. **Clinical Interpretation & ACMG/AMP Guidelines**:
   - Categorization of genomic variants: Pathogenic, Likely Pathogenic, VUS (Variant of Uncertain Significance), Likely Benign, Benign.
   - Population allele frequencies (gnomAD), in silico pathogenicity predictors, and evolutionary conservation scores (phyloP, phastCons).
4. **Phylogenetics & Pathogen Genomics**:
   - Application of molecular evolutionary genetics to infectious disease tracking, vector-borne surveillance, and epidemiological contact networks.
