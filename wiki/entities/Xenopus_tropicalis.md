# *Xenopus tropicalis* (Western Clawed Frog)

## Overview
*Xenopus tropicalis* is a diploid amphibian model organism representing Lissamphibia. In our phylogenetic pipeline, it bridges the evolutionary transition between aquatic teleost fish (*[[Danio_rerio]]*) and terrestrial amniotes (mammals and birds).

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]] Locus**: NCBI Accession `NM_001016853`.
- **Phylogenetic Node**: Diverged from the amniote stem ~350–360 Mya (early Carboniferous).
