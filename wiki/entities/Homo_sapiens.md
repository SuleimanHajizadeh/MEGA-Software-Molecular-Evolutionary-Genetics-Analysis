# *Homo sapiens* (Human)

## Overview
*Homo sapiens* represents the primate/hominid terminal node in our comparative vertebrate genomic analyses.

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]]**: Accession `NM_000088.4` (5,540 bp).
- **Sister Clade**: *Pan troglodytes* (Chimpanzee, divergence ~6–7 Mya).
- **Outgroup Calibration**: Eutherian mammal divergence ~87.5 Mya.
