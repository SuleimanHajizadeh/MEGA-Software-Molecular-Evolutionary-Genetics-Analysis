# *Gallus gallus* (Chicken)

## Overview
*Gallus gallus* (Chicken) is a key avian representative within Archosauria / Sauropsida. In our phylogenetic reconstructions, it represents the sauropsid sister lineage to mammals, providing a critical intermediate calibration node (~310 Mya divergence between Synapsida and Sauropsida).

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]] Locus**: Accession `NM_204297.2` (5,480 bp) / `NM_204775`.
- **Node Position**: Branches sister to the mammalian radiation (*Homo*, *Pan*, *Mus*, *Rattus*, *Bos*) and derived relative to the teleost outgroup (*Danio rerio*).
