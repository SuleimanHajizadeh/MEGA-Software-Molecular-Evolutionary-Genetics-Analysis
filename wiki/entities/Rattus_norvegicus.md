# *Rattus norvegicus* (Brown Rat)

## Overview
*Rattus norvegicus* represents the second rodent taxon in our comparative alignments, diverging from *[[Mus_musculus]]* approximately 12–15 Mya.

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]] Locus**: NCBI Accession `NM_053304.1` (5,491 bp).
- **Phylogenetic Grouping**: Clusters reliably with *Mus musculus* with 100% bootstrap support across ML and NJ models.
