# Trypanosomatida (*Kinetoplastid Parasites*)

## Overview
**Trypanosomatida** is an order of kinetoplastid protozoa characterized by a single flagellum and a unique mitochondrial genome structure (the kinetoplast). The group comprises major human and animal pathogens including *Leishmania* and *Trypanosoma*.

## Life Cycle & Vector Relationships
- **Digenetic Life Cycle**: Alternates between an insect vector (sandflies *[[Phlebotomus_sergenti]]* / *Sergentomyia*) where parasites develop as flagellated promastigotes, and vertebrate hosts (*[[Homo_sapiens]]*, canines, rodents) where they replicate intracellularly as amastigotes.
- **Isolates in Dataset**: Represented by 30 geographic isolates in [[TRO_Seq_Dataset]] spanning human cutaneous lesions, sandfly vector gut dissections, and reservoir tissues.
