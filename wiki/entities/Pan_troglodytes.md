# *Pan troglodytes* (Chimpanzee)

## Overview
*Pan troglodytes* is the closest extant evolutionary relative to *[[Homo_sapiens]]*. In our phylogenetic reconstructions, it forms the immediate sister clade to humans with an estimated divergence time of ~6–7 Mya.

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]] Locus**: NCBI Accession `XM_001170141.4` (5,453 bp) / `XM_016944896`.
- **Sequence Divergence**: Exhibits lowest pairwise distance relative to *Homo sapiens* ($<0.01$ expected substitutions per site in [[Pairwise_Evolutionary_Distance|K2P distance matrix]]).
