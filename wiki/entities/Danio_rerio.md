# *Danio rerio* (Zebrafish)

## Overview
*Danio rerio* is a freshwater teleost fish and premier vertebrate model organism. In our phylogenetic datasets, it serves as the basal vertebrate lineage / outgroup relative to tetrapods, capturing over ~450 million years of evolutionary separation from mammalian lineages.

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]] Locus**: Accession `NM_212864.2` (4,985 bp) / `NM_001004631`.
- **Evolutionary Distance**: Exhibits the largest pairwise divergence relative to eutherian mammals in [[Pairwise_Evolutionary_Distance|JC69 and K2P distance matrices]].
- **Phylogenetic Position**: Basal branch in ML and NJ trees constructed in [[MEGA12]] and [[Phylo_Pipeline_Script|phylo_pipeline.py]].
