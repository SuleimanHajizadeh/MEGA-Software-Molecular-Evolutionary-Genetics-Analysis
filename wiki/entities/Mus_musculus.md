# *Mus musculus* (House Mouse)

## Overview
*Mus musculus* is the primary mammalian model organism in biomedical genetics. In our phylogenetic pipeline, it forms the monophyletic Rodentia / Muridae clade together with *[[Rattus_norvegicus]]*.

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]] Locus**: NCBI Accession `NM_007742.4` (5,495 bp).
- **Evolutionary Rate**: Shows elevated molecular evolutionary rates compared to primates due to shorter generation times, reflected in longer branch lengths in [[Neighbor_Joining_Algorithm|NJ]] and ML trees.
