# *Bos taurus* (Cattle)

## Overview
*Bos taurus* represents the Laurasiatheria / Artiodactyla branch of placental mammals in our phylogenetic dataset, diverging from the Euarchontoglires lineage (primates + rodents) ~87.5 Mya.

## Key Datasets & Loci
- **[[COL1A1]] Locus**: NCBI Accession `NM_174520.3` (5,498 bp).
- **Calibration Role**: Serves as a reference anchor node for Laurasiatheria vs Euarchontoglires divergence in [[Molecular_Clock_RelTime|RelTime molecular dating]].
