# COL1A1 (*Collagen Type I Alpha 1 Chain*)

## Overview
**COL1A1** encodes the pro-alpha1 chain of Type I collagen, the most abundant extracellular matrix protein in vertebrates. Conserved across >450 million years of evolutionary history, its strong phylogenetic signal makes it an exceptional molecular marker for cross-species evolutionary reconstructions.

## Key Attributes & Evolutionary Relevance
- **Conserved Domain**: Triple-helical repeat region containing characteristic `Gly-X-Y` triplets.
- **Evolutionary Depth**: Conserved across jawless/jawed fishes (*Danio rerio*), reptiles/birds (*Gallus gallus*), and mammals (*Homo sapiens*, *Pan troglodytes*, *Mus musculus*, *Rattus norvegicus*, *Bos taurus*).
- **Phylogenetic Utility**: High sequence length (~5,000–5,500 bp coding sequence) and balanced mutation rates enable robust resolution of deep and shallow vertebrate nodes.
