# *Phlebotomus sergenti* (Sandfly Vector)

## Overview
*Phlebotomus sergenti* is a species of phlebotomine sandfly (Diptera: Psychodidae) recognized as the primary biological vector of *Leishmania tropica*, the causative agent of anthroponotic cutaneous leishmaniasis (ACL) in the Old World.

## Epidemiological & Phylogenetic Significance
- **Geographic Range**: Arid and semi-arid regions of the Mediterranean basin, Middle East (Palestine, Iran), and Central Asia.
- **Vector Competence**: Transmits promastigotes between human hosts, rock hyraxes (*Procavia capensis*), and canines (*[[Canis_lupus_familiaris|Canis lupus]]*).
- **Representative Isolates in Repo**: Accessions `EU683617` (West Bank 1998), `HM060588`, `HM060589`, `HM060590` (Iran 2010), `MT966013`, `MT966014`, `MW111284` (Palestine 2020).
